## About Me

I am a first-year PhD candidate at the **HKUST NLP Group**, Hong Kong University of Science and Technology, supervised by **Prof. Junxian He**. I graduated from **Shanghai Jiao Tong University (SJTU)** in June 2024, where I was previously advised by Prof. Junxian He during my undergraduate studies. My research focuses on **natural language processing** and **machine learning**.
